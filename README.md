# Mahendra Connect

Mahendra Connect is a concept homepage for a B2B buyer-supplier community platform. The product direction is relationship-first: buyers discover and contact the right sales person at a supplier company instead of browsing a catalog-based marketplace.

## Product vision

Build a LinkedIn-style professional network for procurement and supplier sales teams where:

- Buyer-company users maintain professional profiles with company role, categories handled, and buying interests.
- Supplier companies create company pages with capabilities, product families, certifications, and sales coverage.
- Supplier sales people are mapped company-wise by category, geography, account, or industry segment.
- Suppliers publish product posts, launch updates, case studies, documents, and buyer Q&A.
- Buyers can search, follow, connect, request introductions, and message relevant sales owners directly.

## MVP modules

1. **Verified companies and members** — onboarding for buyer companies, supplier companies, buyer users, and supplier sales users.
2. **Professional profiles** — LinkedIn-like profiles for buyers and sales people with role, location, company, categories, and authority.
3. **Supplier pages** — supplier company profiles that showcase capabilities and assign sales contacts to segments.
4. **Product update feed** — posts for product launches, datasheets, certifications, sample availability, and discussions.
5. **Relevant contact matching** — routing rules that recommend the right sales person based on category, region, industry, company assignment, or relationship history.
6. **Direct outreach workflow** — connection requests, introductions, messages, and meeting requests.
7. **Company-wise sales tracking** — admin visibility into supplier sales coverage, buyer interactions, response activity, and account ownership.

## Current implementation

The repository currently contains a static landing page in `index.html` that explains the platform concept, features, workflow, and MVP next step.

Core modules

Everything needed for a relationship-first supplier network.

Focus on verified people, company context, product conversations, and measurable supplier engagement.

👤

Buyer & sales profiles

Maintain LinkedIn-like professional profiles with role, company, categories handled, location, and decision authority.

🏢

Supplier company pages

Let suppliers showcase capabilities, product families, certifications, plants, and the sales team responsible for each segment.

📣

Product posts & updates

Enable supplier posts for launches, samples, case studies, pricing signals, technical documents, and buyer Q&A.

🎯

Relevant contact matching

Route buyers to the correct sales person based on industry, geography, category, company assignment, or existing relationship.

📊

Company-wise sales tracking

Track which sales people represent which suppliers, buyer interactions, response activity, and account coverage gaps.

💬

Direct outreach

Support connection requests, introductions, messages, meeting requests, and warm handoffs without turning the site into a cart.

How it works

A simple discovery-to-conversation flow.

1

Verify companies

Approve buyer companies, supplier companies, and users before they join the trusted network.

2

Map people

Attach buyers and sales people to companies, categories, territories, and account ownership.

3

Publish updates

Suppliers post product stories and buyers follow the categories or vendors relevant to their work.

4

Start outreach

Buyers request introductions or message the right sales owner with full company context.

Next build step
